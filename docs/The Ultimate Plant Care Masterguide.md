## 🪴Indoor Tropicals

#### 🌿Calathea Ornata (Pinstripe Plant)

**Light:** Bright, indirect light. Direct sun bleaches the pink stripes and scorches the thin leaf tissue.

**Water:** Keep soil consistently moist but not waterlogged. Use rainwater or distilled water to avoid fluoride/chlorine tip-burn.

**Humidity:** High (50%+). Group with other plants or use a pebble tray. Mist frequently.

**Expert Advice:** Known as a 'Prayer Plant,' it moves its leaves up at night. If leaves stay vertical all day, it's likely receiving too much light. New leaves appear as tight 'cigars'—do not touch or force them open as they are highly delicate.

**Where is it:** On north-West Facing kitchen windowsill, in a small plantpot with no drainage.

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Brown, crispy edges usually indicate low humidity or chemical sensitivity in tap water. Yellow leaves often mean overwatering.*

#### 🌿Alocasia Zebrina (Zebra Plant)

**Light:** Very bright, indirect light. These long stems are highly phototropic—rotate the pot 90 degrees every few days to prevent a heavy lean.

**Water:** Allow the top 25-50% of soil to dry out. Alocasias grow from corms (bulbs) that rot easily in soggy soil.

**Humidity:** High. Enjoys a humid kitchen or bathroom. Wipe large leaves regularly to prevent dust buildup and spider mites.

**Expert Advice:** Follows a 'one in, one out' rule: the plant often sacrifices its oldest, smallest leaf at the base to provide energy for a massive new center spear. If you see water droplets on leaf tips (guttation), you are overwatering.

**Where is it:** On South-East facing living room, near large glass patio doors, in a medium sized plantpot with drainage.

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Drooping stems usually mean it’s thirsty, but if the soil is wet, it’s a sign of root rot.*

## 🪴Indoor Succulents

#### 🌿Snake Plant (Sansevieria)

**Light:** The ultimate low-light survivor, but thrives in bright indirect light. Avoid mid-day direct sun in hot windows.

**Water:** Water deeply but infrequently (every 3-4 weeks). Soil must be bone-dry through the entire pot.

**Humidity:** Unfussy. Thrives in standard indoor dry air.

**Expert Advice:** A champion at air purification, producing oxygen at night. It actually prefers to be 'root-bound' (tight in its pot).

**Where is it:** On north-West Facing kitchen windowsill.

⚠️***Warning Signs:** If yellow mushrooms (Flowerpot Parasols) appear in the soil, the soil is staying damp for too long. Pluck them and let the plant dry out completely.*

#### 🌿Mother of Thousands (Kalanchoe)

**Light:** Full direct sun. Needs high light to prevent it from becoming 'leggy' or spindly.

**Water:** Succulent care: water deeply, then let soil dry out 100%. Targeted watering near the stem is best for young plants.

**Humidity:** Low. Keep away from humidifiers.

**Expert Advice:** Produces hundreds of tiny plantlets on leaf edges. They fall off and root easily. **Extremely toxic** to pets and children due to cardiac glycosides. Handle with care.

**Where is it:** On north-West Facing kitchen windowsill.

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Stretching stems mean it is starving for light. Move to your brightest south-facing window. Outdoor Hanging/Trailing*


## 🌼Outdoor Perennials

#### 🌿Erysimum 'Bowles’s Mauve' (Wallflower)

**Light:** Full sun. Essential for maintaining the vibrant purple flower spikes.

**Water:** Well-drained soil is vital. They hate sitting in wet, cold soil over a Welsh winter.

**Expert Advice:** A prolific bloomer. Deadhead regularly by cutting the entire flower stalk back to the first set of leaves once the petals drop to encourage a continuous flush.

**Where is it:** South-West Facing Garden Planter, with a high fence behind.

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Do not prune hard into the 'old wood' (the brown, leafless base). It rarely regrows from old wood; keep trims light and green.*

#### 🌿Hydrangea (Mophead)

**Light:** Morning sun and afternoon shade. The broad leaves transpire water quickly and will wilt in the afternoon heat.

**Water:** A water-glutton. Keep soil consistently damp. Rainwater is best for maintaining health.

**Expert Advice:** Color is determined by soil pH. Deep blue indicates acidic soil. Use ericaceous (acid-loving) compost and feed to keep them blue.

**Where is it:** in a small (around 20l) pot against the south-east fence. Direct sunlight from midday to early evening. 

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Wilted leaves in the afternoon don't always mean it's dry; it’s a cooling mechanism. Check the soil before drowning it.*

## 🥔Outdoor Edibles

#### 🌿Potatoes

**Light:** Full sun to help the foliage produce sugars.

**Water:** Keep consistently moist once they begin to flower, as this is when the tubers are bulking up.

**Expert Advice:** Do not prune the greenery! The leaves are 'solar panels' feeding the potatoes underground. Switch to a high-potash (tomato) feed once established to focus energy on the roots rather than leaves.

**Where is it:** South-West Facing Garden Planter, with a high fence behind. 

⚠️***Warning Signs:** If leaves turn green/yellow and die back naturally, it’s time to harvest. If they turn black suddenly, check for 'blight' after wet weather.*

#### 🌿Strawberries

**Light:** Full sun for the sweetest fruit.

**Water:** Water the base, not the leaves, to prevent mold/mildew. Keep soil damp.

**Expert Advice:** Mulch with straw or use 'strawberry mats' to keep the fruit off the wet soil. This prevents rot and deters slugs. Feed with tomato fertilizer every 2 weeks during fruiting.

**Where is it:** South-West Facing Garden Planter, with a high fence behind. 

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Birds love red berries—use netting once the fruit begins to ripen. 

## 🌳Outdoor Trees & Shrubs*

#### 🌿Pear Tree

**Light:** Full sun in a sheltered spot to protect the blossoms from late frosts.

**Water:** Crucial during the first 3 years and while fruit is swelling.

**Expert Advice:** Watch for 'Pear Rust'—bright orange spots on the leaves in summer. Pick off affected leaves if seen early. Annual winter pruning is needed to keep the shape and encourage fruiting wood.

**Where is it:** in a very large (over 100l) pot against the south-east fence. Direct sunlight from midday to late evening. 

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Small, shriveled fruit often means the tree was too dry during the 'June drop' period.*

#### 🌿Acer (Japanese Maple)

**Light:** Dappled shade. Mid-day sun can burn the delicate, thin leaves.

**Water:** Needs consistent moisture. Do not let it dry out completely in a pot.

**Expert Advice:** The #1 enemy is **wind**. Wind causes 'leaf scorch' where the edges turn brown and crispy. Keep in a sheltered corner of the garden.

**Where is it:** South-West Facing Garden Planter, with a high fence behind. In a shaded corner protected from wind.

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Crispy leaf tips in Cardiff are almost always wind damage rather than a watering issue.*

#### 🌿Olive Tree

**Light:** The hottest, sunniest spot you have.

**Water:** Drought tolerant once established, but potted olives need regular watering. Must have gritty, free-draining soil.

**Expert Advice:** Very hardy, but hates 'wet feet' in winter. If your garden is boggy, keep the olive in a pot on 'feet' (pot risers) to help it drain.

**Where is it:** in a planter near the center if the garden. Shaded during low sun but direct sunlight during high sun.

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Yellowing/dropping leaves in winter usually mean the roots are too cold and wet.*

#### 🌿Oak (Sapling)

**Light:** Full sun. They are light-hungry from a young age.

**Water:** Deep watering is required to encourage the taproot to move downward.

**Expert Advice:** A legacy plant. If in a pot, it will require a significantly larger container every 2 years. Beware of 'Powdery Mildew' on the leaves in late summer (white dusty coating).

**Where is it:** in a planter near the center if the garden. Shaded during low sun but direct sunlight during high sun.

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Avoid moving it once planted in the ground, as the taproot is very sensitive to disturbance.*

#### 🌿New Zealand Flax (Phormium)

**Light:** Full sun to partial shade. Very hardy.

**Water:** Low maintenance once established. Does well in coastal or windy Cardiff conditions.

**Expert Advice:** Structural and architectural. Maintenance is simple: reach to the base of dead or tattered outer leaves and pull firmly to remove them.

**Where is it:** in a medium (around 30l) pot against the south-east fence. Direct sunlight from midday to late evening. 

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Relatively pest-free, but check the base for mealybugs in very dense clumps.*

## 🏵️Outdoor Climbing/Feature

#### 🌿Jasmine

**Light:** Full sun for the best scent and flower production.

**Water:** Keep soil moist but not soggy.

**Expert Advice:** Needs a support structure (trellis/wire). Prune right after flowering to keep it from becoming a tangled 'birds nest.'

**Where is it:** South-West Facing Garden Planter, with a high fence behind. 

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Lack of flowers usually means it isn't getting enough hours of direct sunlight.*

## 🌻Outdoor Annuals

#### 🌿Sunflowers

**Light:** 6-8 hours of direct, unobstructed sun.

**Water:** Heavy drinkers. They will wilt quickly if the soil dries out, especially in black plastic pots.

**Expert Advice:** Protect seeds with mesh until they are at least 4 inches tall to stop birds and squirrels. Feed with high-potash fertilizer once the bud appears to get the largest possible flower head.

**Where is it:** currently seeds in many small compostable germination pots. Against south-east fence. Direct sunlight from midday to early evening. 

⚠️***Warning Signs:** Slugs love young sunflower stalks—use grit or copper tape around the pots.*
